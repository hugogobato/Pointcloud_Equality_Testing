# Phase 3 implementation and calibration note

Phase 3's estimator and calibration layer is implemented in
`tda2s/tests/dr_outcome.py`. The AIPW estimate remains delegated to
`tcda_uq.estimators.cross_fit`, including its function-on-scalar outcome
regressions, propensity model, cross-fitting, and per-unit scores. The new
layer reconstructs the out-of-fold predictions once from the returned fold
objects and checks that the cached score tensor agrees with `tcda_uq` to
numerical precision.

The primary statistic is the shared-degree max-over-scale sup norm,
`sqrt(n) max_d sup_t |psi_hat_d(t)|`. The implementation also exposes raw L2,
studentized sup, and studentized L2 diagnostics, positivity and effective
sample-size diagnostics, a sup-norm equivalence decision, and a propensity
learner grid. The double-robustness driver uses a nine-term oracle outcome
model and deliberately misspecifies the Fourier basis and/or the two
propensity interactions.

Degree multiplicity has explicit Bonferroni and shared max-statistic
calibrations, and, since Phase 3.5, a studentized empirical-null comparator
adapted from Vejdemo-Johansson and Mukherjee (`vjm_multiplicity_test`). The
mapping audit passed as an adaptation rather than an import: the source's
point-process null model, the independence of its null draws, and its
null-only standardization are all replaced, and the transfer is justified by
exchangeability of P1's own conditional null together with Kim and Lee's
functional CLT rather than by the source's persistent-Betti-number limit
theorem. The full audit, including a correction to the section numbers the
research plan cites, is `docs/phase3_5_vjm_mapping.md`.

The unresolved Phase 2 calibration issue is handled by a cached,
vectorized, covariate-preserving permutation path. It permutes labels only
within supplied propensity strata and evaluates the AIPW score with fixed
out-of-fold nuisances. Thus 40 permutations no longer mean 40 fits. This is
finite-sample exact only when the nuisance fits and strata are fixed
independently of the permuted labels, such as a sharp conditional null with
externally fixed design information. With estimated label-dependent nuisances
it is a fast cross-fitted calibration approximation, and the code reports that
scope explicitly rather than claiming exactness.

The long experiments are in `experiments/phase3_dr_calibration.py`. The
oracle functional fleet covers `n in {50, 100, 200, 500}`, three propensity
regimes, null and alternative outcomes, and 500 replications per cell. The
point-cloud, learner, and double-robustness runs are separate designs. The
self-contained Colab fleets are generated by
`experiments/colab/make_phase3_notebooks.py` and download one JSON checkpoint
per shard.

The local checks passed the cached-fit invariants, shared-multiplier
reproducibility, strata-count preservation, positivity diagnostics, and
oracle smoke path. A small local null feasibility run also reproduces the
known sensitivity of finite-sample rejection rates to `n` and statistic form;
it is intentionally not treated as a Phase 3 gate because it has far fewer
than 500 replications. The full oracle fleet is now aggregated; the remaining
formal work is the strict calibration review and Phase 3.5 mapping decision.

## Downloaded result checkpoint (2026-08-15)

The downloaded JSON checkpoints are in `experiments/colab/` and aggregate into
`results/phase3_oracle_summary.json`, `results/phase3_clouds_summary.json`,
`results/phase3_learners_summary.json`, and
`results/phase3_stress_summary.json`. Duplicate `(1)` downloads are ignored
only when their replication-cell records are byte-identical; conflicting
records raise an error.

The point-cloud fleet has 200 replications in each of its two cells. Its null
rejection rates are 0.035 for the multiplier test and 0.025 for the frozen
stratified permutation test. The learner fleet has 100 replications per
learner, with null rates 0.03--0.06 for multiplier and 0.03--0.04 for
permutation. The stress fleet has 100 replications per misspecification case;
its null rates range from 0.00 to 0.05 for multiplier and 0.00 to 0.03 for
permutation.

The oracle fleet now has 500 replications in every one of its 24 cells.
Across the null cells, multiplier rejection rates range from 0.0260 to 0.0640
and permutation rejection rates from 0.0260 to 0.0520. Alternative rejection
rates range from 0.9900 to 1.000 for multiplier and 0.9440 to 1.000 for
permutation. The two 0.0260 null cells have Monte Carlo standard errors of
approximately 0.0071, so they are within Monte Carlo error of the 0.03 lower
target, but the strict pre-registered band is recorded rather than silently
rounded. These results support the cached permutation implementation; the
formal Phase 3 exit remains open for the calibration decision and Phase 3.5.

The seven missing oracle shards were run locally with seven independent
workers, one thread per worker, and completed in about sixteen minutes. The
environment exposed eight CPUs, so one was left free; peak memory stayed well
below the 12 GB limit.

## Phase 3.5 comparator fleet (2026-08-16)

`experiments/phase3_5_vjm.py` adds the degree-multiplicity benchmark. Its
`fwer` design reuses the Phase 3 oracle null design and every Phase 3 seed, so
its shared max-statistic column reproduces the published Phase 3 `permutation_p`
and `multiplier_p` exactly; `--mode check-phase3` verifies this on 36 of 36
null cells and a reduced version runs in `tests/test_phase3_5_vjm.py`. The
comparator columns are therefore a strict addition to the Phase 3 draws rather
than an independent rerun, and the four multiplicity procedures are compared on
one shared null matrix per replication, with no Monte Carlo noise between them.

The Colab fleet is eight notebooks generated by
`experiments/colab/make_phase3_5_notebooks.py`: four for the 500-replication
`fwer` decision and one each for `power`, `degrees3`, `learners` and `stress`.
Measured local replication costs are about 12 s (`fwer`), 4 s (`power` and
`degrees3`), 5 s (`learners`) and 6 s (`stress`), which puts every notebook
inside roughly 25 minutes. Shards are 25 replications each so a dropped
session costs one checkpoint. The fleet has not been run yet; the Phase 3.5
exit rule and the pending steps are in `docs/phase3_5_vjm_mapping.md` §4.
