## Computational qualification

SC-A uses a predeclared 250-point projection per arm once the supplied cloud exceeds 250 points. This makes the full permutation baseline feasible, but its effective point sample size is 250 and its power must not be compared to SC-B or SC-C as if all methods used the same number of points. SC-B uses one frozen disjoint partition and reports `K_a=floor(n_a/m)`. SC-C uses the full clouds and the frozen smoothed-bootstrap count. No overlapping block is used in the confirmatory SC-B path. The 100-replication pilot screened all three candidates, and the completed 500-replication density screen reports rejection rates 0.514/0.450 for SC-A at n=250/500, 0.994/1.000 for SC-B, and 0.122/0.006 for SC-C; the SC-B rates confirm that the cell is measure-sensitive, and the SC-C n=500 rate is the conservative tail of its finite-vector bootstrap. The final hard gate is scoped to SC-B because it is the only candidate whose declared target matches `H0,25^bar`; the pilot showed SC-C to be conservative with no detectable topology power, and SC-A remains a non-target-matched baseline rather than a competing production method.

## Interpretation ledger

The translated-law weak null is a direct barcode-law witness: the two point laws differ, but translations preserve all metric Vietoris-Rips diagrams. The density-shift cell is not a null for the locked metric-measure target, even though its support topology is unchanged. Rejection in that cell therefore diagnoses the target's measure sensitivity, not a topological-type discovery. The topology alternative compares a filled disk with a noisy circle at matched cardinality and matched expected center and scale.

The contamination stress cell is conservative: SC-B rejection is 0.004/0.000/0.006 at n=250/500/1000, below the size band, which is reported as a robustness diagnostic rather than a size claim.

The overlap panel is a negative control whose nominal fractions 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9 realize only 0.0%, 2.4%, 4.8%, 15.4%, 32.7% mean pairwise block reuse, with rejection rates 0.068, 0.084, 0.082, 0.260, 0.426; even the smallest realized reuse already pushes the rate above the size band. Its intentionally reused points do not create independent barcode draws, so this departure from the size band is evidence against pseudo-replicated inference, not a production result. Process and spatial dependence families remain dormant because Phase 5A selected Regime I and no SC-D candidate was admitted to the fleet.

## Phase 5D hardening record

Phase 5D is complete for SC-B. The audit identified that the prototype's
averaged degree kernel was characteristic only for the two degree marginals,
not for their joint law. The production implementation now uses the
tensor-product degree kernel. The finite-sample permutation argument and its
conditional assumptions are recorded in `theory/WP5D_SC-B_hardening.md`, and
the frozen production contract is documented in `docs/phase5d_scb_usage.md`.

The corrected-kernel rerun used 500 independent replications per cell and 39
Monte Carlo label permutations per replication. SC-B rejection rates for the
basic iid null at $n=250,500,1000$ were $0.050,0.068,0.042$. The translated
weak barcode-law null gave $0.052,0.054,0.046$. The topology alternative gave
power $0.998,1.000,1.000$. The replication-level output is
`results/phase5d_scb_hardening_500.parquet`.

The production API uses one frozen disjoint partition, reports
$K_a=\lfloor n_a/25\rfloor$, requires at least five complete blocks per arm,
and refuses unsupported regimes, data-dependent partitions, and repeated
partition aggregation. A future Bonferroni aggregation over a predeclared
finite family of partitions is a possible conservative extension, but it is
not part of the current production claim.
